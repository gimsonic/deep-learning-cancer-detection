ABSTRACT

Cancer remains one of the leading causes of death worldwide, and early detection plays a critical role in improving patient survival rates and treatment outcomes. However, conventional cancer diagnosis often relies on manual interpretation of medical images by specialists, which can be time-consuming, costly, and subject to human error. These challenges are particularly significant in regions where access to expert healthcare professionals is limited. To address this issue, this project proposes the development of an AI-Based Multi-Cancer Detection System using deep learning techniques.
The proposed system focuses on the detection of four common cancer types: breast cancer, skin cancer, oral cancer and lung cancer. Medical image datasets are collected from reliable open-source repositories and preprocessed using techniques such as resizing, normalization, augmentation, and noise reduction to improve data quality and model performance. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) combined with transfer learning approaches are employed to develop accurate and efficient classification models. Pre-trained architectures such as MobileNet and ResNet are utilized to reduce training time while maintaining high detection accuracy.
The trained models are evaluated using standard performance metrics including accuracy, precision, recall, sensitivity, specificity, and Area Under the Curve (AUC). To improve interpretability and user trust, visualization techniques such as Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) are incorporated to highlight image regions influencing model predictions. Furthermore, a lightweight web-based prototype is developed, allowing users to upload medical images and receive AI-generated diagnostic predictions through an intuitive interface.
The expected outcome of this project is a reliable, scalable, and accessible cancer detection framework that supports early diagnosis and assists healthcare professionals in decision-making. This system also provides a foundation for future expansion to additional cancer types and broader integration of artificial intelligence technologies within the healthcare sector.

Limitations
Although the proposed AI-Based Multi-Cancer Detection System aims to provide accurate and efficient cancer detection, several limitations exist due to resource constraints, data availability, and project scope. The following limitations apply to the current implementation of the system.

Limited Cancer Types
The system focuses only on breast cancer, skin cancer, oral cancer and lung cancer. Other cancer types are not included within the scope of this project.

Image-Based Detection Only
The system performs analysis solely based on medical images. Other clinical information such as patient history, laboratory reports, and genetic factors are not considered.

Not a Replacement for Medical Experts
The system is designed to provide diagnostic support and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or clinical decision-making.

Limited Validation Data
Model evaluation is performed using available validation and test datasets. Real-world clinical validation with large-scale patient data is beyond the scope of this project.

Internet Dependency
Since the system is deployed as a web-based application, users require an internet connection to access its functionality.

Computational Resource Constraints
Training deep learning models requires significant computational resources. The performance of the system may be limited by the available hardware and cloud computing resources used during development.
