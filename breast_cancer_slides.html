"Hi everyone, I'm [Your Name], and I'm responsible for the Breast Cancer Detection module in CurieSense AI.

For our model, we chose ResNet50 with transfer learning pre-trained on ImageNet, then fine-tuned specifically on mammogram ROI patches. The input is grayscale at 224×224, enhanced using CLAHE — adaptive contrast enhancement — to match exactly how the model was trained.

We trained in three progressive phases: first training only the classification head, then gradually unfreezing the top 30 layers, and finally the top 60 layers — this avoids destroying the pretrained features too early.

As you can see in the table, we've gone through four model versions. Our best so far — DS_M2 v4.3 — achieves 74.3% validation accuracy and an AUC of 0.819, up from 0.615 in our very first version."

Slide 2 — Achievements & Challenges (~55 sec)
⏱ ~0:55

"On the achievements side — CLAHE preprocessing is fully integrated and applied to every patch at inference time, ensuring consistency with training. We also implemented a sliding-window approach to handle full-size mammograms, since our model is trained on small patches, not whole images.

The biggest challenge was contrast variation — different patches had wildly different brightness levels. We solved that with dual-pass adaptive CLAHE. The second challenge was making full-image inference work — we used a sliding window across the mammogram, then Top-K Mean aggregation to combine patch-level predictions into one final result.

Both models are deployed and the pipeline is fully functional end-to-end. Thank you."
